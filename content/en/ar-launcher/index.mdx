# AR Launcher

AR Launcher is the program you use to join FiveM servers that run AR Team's system. Keep it open the whole
time you are playing. Pick a server and press **Connect** in the launcher, and it opens the game for you.

## Before you install [#requirements]

Your PC needs all of these:

- **Windows only**
- **GTA V and FiveM installed**
- **Your Steam account linked in FiveM's settings**, the same account you sign in to AR Launcher with

<Callout type="info">
  If you have just installed FiveM, open it yourself once, wait for its main screen, then close it before
  you press Connect in AR Launcher for the first time. Otherwise the launcher may not find FiveM.
</Callout>
