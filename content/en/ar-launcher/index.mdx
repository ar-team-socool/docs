# AR Launcher

AR Launcher is the program you use to join FiveM and RedM servers that run AR Team's system. Keep it open
the whole time you are playing. Pick a server and press **Connect** in AR Launcher, and it opens the game for you.

## Before you install [#requirements]

Your PC needs all of these:

- **Windows only**
- **GTA V and FiveM installed** for a FiveM server, or **Red Dead Redemption 2 and RedM** for a RedM server
- **Your Steam account linked in FiveM's or RedM's settings** (the game you play), the same account you sign in
  to AR Launcher with

<Callout type="info">
  If you have just installed FiveM or RedM, open that game yourself once, wait for its main screen, then close
  it before you press Connect in AR Launcher for the first time. Otherwise AR Launcher may not find the game.
</Callout>
