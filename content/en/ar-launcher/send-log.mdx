# Send your log file

The log file records what AR Launcher did on your PC. The team uses it to find the cause of a problem.

<Steps>

### Open the log folder [#open-folder]

Press <kbd>Windows</kbd> + <kbd>R</kbd>, paste this, then press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

```text
%APPDATA%\AR Launcher\logs
```

### Find the files [#find-files]

The folder holds **launcher.log**, and may also hold **launcher.old.log**.

### Send them to the team [#send]

Send both files to the team of the server you play on, and tell them what happened and roughly when.
Include AR Launcher's version number too. It is at the top left of the window, next to the name AR LAUNCHER.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  Send the files to the team directly. Do not post them in a public chat.
</Callout>
