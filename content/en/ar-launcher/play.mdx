# Joining a server

## Open AR Launcher [#open-launcher]

Every time AR Launcher opens, Windows asks with a yellow **Publisher: Unknown** prompt. Choose **Yes**.
The permission is for the anti-cheat only. AR Launcher and the game do not get it, and the game starts the
same way as when you open it yourself. Choose **Yes** only if you installed it from
[the places named on the install page](/en/ar-launcher/install#download).

With No, you cannot join a server that checks for the anti-cheat until you choose **Yes** when Windows asks
again as you press Connect. If you cannot see the prompt, look for the flashing icon on the taskbar.

## Choose a server [#choose-server]

The **Servers** page has three tabs.

- **All**: every server, with the recommended one as a large card at the top.
- **Recent**: the servers you joined through AR Launcher on this PC, the latest first.
- **Favorites**: the servers you starred. Press the star again to take one off.

Favorites and recent servers are kept on this PC, for each Steam account.

### Search, filter and sort [#find-server]

On the **All** tab, icon buttons sit at the right of the tab row. Hover over one to see its name.

- **Search servers** (magnifying glass): type a server's name or short name. Press Esc to clear it.
- **Filter** (funnel): tick a box to hide servers that are offline, empty or full. The number beside each
  box is how many servers it hides. When there are both FiveM and RedM servers, you can hide either game.
- **Sort** (arrows): choose **Recommended**, **Players** or **Server name**. Press the same one again to
  reverse the order.
- **Show as cards** or **Show as a list**: AR Launcher remembers your choice on this PC.

While you search, filter or sort, the page shows only the servers that match. Press **Clear filters** to
go back to the normal page. Each time AR Launcher opens, the filters start empty.

## Connect to a server [#connect]

Pick a server and press **Connect**, then let AR Launcher open the game.
The **Connect** button carries the logo of the server's game, FiveM or RedM.

<Callout type="warning">
  Do not start FiveM or RedM yourself. On some servers AR Launcher closes a game it did not open.
</Callout>

### If FiveM or RedM is already open [#fivem-already-open]

AR Launcher cannot start the game while FiveM or RedM is open, and shows **FiveM or RedM is open**.
Press **Close the game and connect** and AR Launcher closes the game and connects you to the server.
A game in progress will be closed.

### Cancel while connecting [#cancel-join]

While AR Launcher is starting or connecting, press **Cancel connecting**, then **Cancel and close FiveM**
(**Cancel and close RedM** on a RedM server). AR Launcher closes the game and stops connecting.
You can then pick a server and press **Connect** again.

## While you play [#while-playing]

<Callout type="error">
  **Keep AR Launcher open while you play.** If it is closed, crashes or loses its connection, the server
  may kick you out of the game.
</Callout>

Once you have joined a server, AR Launcher moves to the notification area at the bottom right of the
taskbar, next to the clock, and has no taskbar button. It is still running.

- Click the AR Launcher icon to open the window. If you cannot see the icon, click the **^** arrow next
  to the clock.
- Minimize the window and AR Launcher goes back there.

When you leave the server, AR Launcher closes itself and the game stays open. If you were kicked or the game
crashed, the game's message stays on screen for you to read. To play again, open AR Launcher. If the game
is still open, see [If FiveM or RedM is already open](#fivem-already-open).

## Updates [#updates]

Install it once. When a new version comes out, AR Launcher updates on its own, so there is no need to
download it again.

If you are playing on a server, restarting to update disconnects you immediately, and you will need to
reconnect.
